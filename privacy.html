Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Loads Laundry Co. ("Loads," "we") offers laundry pickup and delivery in Los Angeles. This policy explains what we collect when you use our website or services, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Booking details you give us: name, mobile number, pickup address, zip code, pickup notes, and how you heard about us.
  • Waitlist details: zip code and mobile number.
  • Order details: pickup and delivery dates, weight, order total and whether it's paid.
  • Payment details: card payments are handled by Square. If you save a card, Square stores it; we never see or keep your full card number.
  • Website and ad data: the page you arrived on, the site or ad that sent you (including ad click IDs), your browser type, and similar information collected by cookies and tags from Google and Meta.

How we use it

  • To schedule, pick up, clean and deliver your laundry, and to contact you about your order.
  • To send reminders and offers by text, only if you opted in.
  • To understand which ads and channels bring customers, and to improve our ads and website.

Text messages

If you book, we text you about that order. If you also opt in to reminders and offers, we send up to 4 marketing texts a month. Reply STOP to stop texts, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text opt-in with third parties for their marketing.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run the business, such as Google (website analytics, ads and the spreadsheet where bookings are stored), Meta (ads measurement) and our texting provider [TEXTING PROVIDER]. They may only use your information to provide services to us. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and ads

Our site uses Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel to measure visits and bookings. You can limit ad tracking in your browser and device settings, at Google Ad Settings and in your Facebook ad preferences.

Your choices and California rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and opt out of texts at any time. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. To make a request, contact us below. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and order records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax requirements, then delete them.
