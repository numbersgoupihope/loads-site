Terms of Service

Last updated: November 24, 2026

These terms cover laundry pickup and delivery by Squeaky Clean Ventures, LLC, doing business as Loads ("Loads," "we"). By booking, you agree to them.

The service

We pick up your laundry, wash, dry and fold it at our own laundromats, and return it to the address you gave us. We currently serve zip codes within about 30 miles of our laundromats in Koreatown and Van Nuys. The booking form shows whether we serve your zip.

Prices

  • Wash and fold: $2.25 per pound, weighed at our store before washing.
  • Pickup and delivery: $6.99 for the pickup and $6.99 for the drop-off ($13.98 per order), unless a promotion says otherwise.
  • Minimum order: $30 for wash and fold, before the pickup and delivery fees. Orders that weigh in under the minimum are charged $30.

Loads Weekly (monthly plan)

  • Loads Weekly costs $200 a month. When you sign up on Square's checkout page, your card is charged right away and then once a month on the same day, until you cancel.
  • The plan includes one pickup and drop-off each week (Monday to Sunday) with up to 30 lb of wash and fold. Delivery on that order is $6.99.
  • If that week's pickup weighs more than 30 lb, the extra pounds are $2.25 each. Any additional pickup in the same week is $1.75 per pound plus $6.99 delivery. These charges go on the card saved with your plan after each order is weighed.
  • Pounds you don't use in a week don't carry over to the next week or month.
  • Your plan is linked to the mobile number you signed up with. Book each pickup on our website with that number.
  • You can cancel anytime by texting or emailing us. Cancelling stops future monthly charges; your plan stays active until the end of the month you already paid for.

Payment

You don't need a card to book. After we weigh your laundry, we text you the weight and your total. On your first order we send an invoice with a payment link through Square, and you pay it by card before we deliver.

When you pay, you can choose to save your card with us. If you do, you authorize us to charge that card for your future orders, for the weighed total, after each order is weighed. We text you a receipt every time. You can ask us to remove your saved card at any time by texting or emailing us.

We deliver your order once it's paid. Prices may change; we will show current prices on our website.

Pickup and delivery

Leave your laundry in a closed bag at the spot you named when booking, by the start of your pickup window. If we can't access the location or find your bag, we'll text you to reschedule. When we deliver, we leave your order where you asked. Once delivered to that spot, the order is your responsibility.

What we don't accept

Items that need dry cleaning or special care, items soiled with hazardous materials, and anything in pockets we are asked to return. Check pockets before you bag your laundry. We aren't responsible for items left in pockets, or for damage to items that weren't safe to machine wash and dry.

If something goes wrong

If something comes back wrong, text us within 48 hours and we'll rewash it free. If an item is lost or damaged while in our care, our liability is limited to 10 times the per-pound charge for that item, up to $250 per order.

Cancellations

You can cancel or reschedule by text any time before your pickup window starts, at no charge.

Text messages

By booking, you agree to receive texts about your order. Reminder and offer texts are sent only if you opt in, up to 4 a month. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. See our privacy policy.

Changes

We may update these terms. The version on our website applies to bookings made after the update.
