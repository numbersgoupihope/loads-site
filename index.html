Questions? We've got loads.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. Leave your bag outside your door, or tell us where to find it when you book.

When do I get it back?

Back . You pick a two-hour window for pickup and another for drop-off, any time from 9am to midnight. We text you when the driver is about an hour away.

How do you charge?

No card needed to book. We weigh your laundry dry at the store, before washing, and charge $ a pound with a $ minimum, plus $ each way for pickup and drop-off. A full hamper usually comes in around 10 lbs. We text you the weight, a photo of the scale and your total.

First order: you get a secure payment link from Square. Tick “save card” when you pay, and after that we just charge your saved card, so there's nothing to do. Your laundry comes back as soon as it's paid.

How does Loads Weekly work?

It's $ a month, charged to your card by Square. Each week, one pickup of up to lb is included, and delivery on that order is $. If that pickup weighs more, only the extra pounds are charged, at $ each. A second pickup in the same week is $ a pound plus $ delivery. Unused pounds don't roll over. Book each pickup on this page like normal, and cancel anytime by text or email.
